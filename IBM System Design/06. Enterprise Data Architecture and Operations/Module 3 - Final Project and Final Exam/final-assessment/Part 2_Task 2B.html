1. Executive Summary & Architectural Drivers

The DigiHealth Enterprise Data Architecture modernization initiative transitions the healthcare system to a high-performance OLAP Data Warehouse:

🔗 1. Eliminate Data Silos
Unifies transactional data across clinical, financial, and administrative hospital departments into a single source of truth.
⚡ 2. Workload Decoupling
Isolates resource-intensive analytical queries from the MySQL OLTP engine, eliminating transaction latency.
📊 3. Advanced BI & Analytics
Enables multi-dimensional slice-and-dice reporting on clinical throughput, revenue cycle, and physician productivity.
🔒 4. Governance & Compliance
Ensures end-to-end security compliance (HIPAA/GDPR) via Role-Based Access Control, PHI tokenization, and audit logging.

3. Fact & Dimension Tables Specification

Table Name Type Keys Description & Purpose
dim_patient Dimension patient_key (Surrogate PK)
patient_id (Natural Key)		 Patient demographic profiles with age brackets and tokenized PHI fields for HIPAA-compliant clinical analytics.
dim_doctor Dimension doctor_key (Surrogate PK)
doctor_id (Natural Key)		 Physician credentials, specialty areas, and associated hospital department hierarchies.
dim_date Dimension date_key (Integer YYYYMMDD) Pre-calculated calendar table with day, month, quarter, and weekend flags for high-speed time-series analysis.
dim_service Dimension service_key (Surrogate PK) Standardized catalog of medical consultations, treatments, procedures, and baseline fees.
fact_appointments Fact Table appointment_fact_id (PK)
Dimension FKs		 Granular metrics per encounter: consultation_fee, total_billed_amount, visit_duration_minutes, appointment_count.

5. Data Governance, Security & Compliance Controls

👥 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Clinical staff access anonymized aggregates; Financial teams access billing dimensions; System DBAs retain administrative privileges.
🛡 HIPAA & GDPR Compliance
Transparent Data Encryption (AES-256) at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and pseudonymization of direct patient identifiers.
