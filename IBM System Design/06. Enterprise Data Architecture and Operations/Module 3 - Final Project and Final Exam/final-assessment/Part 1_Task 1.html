2. Normalization Strategy & Integrity Analysis

1NF • Atomicity & Unique Keys
Atomic attributes, elimination of repeating columns, single unique primary key per table.
2NF • Full Functional Dependency
Elimination of partial dependencies; all attributes strictly depend on full primary keys.
3NF • Transitive Decoupling
Elimination of transitive dependencies ($X ightarrow Y ightarrow Z$); departments isolated to prevent anomalies.
