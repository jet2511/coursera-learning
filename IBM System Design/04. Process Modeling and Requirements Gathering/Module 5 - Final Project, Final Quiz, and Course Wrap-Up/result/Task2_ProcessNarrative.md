## Process Narrative Matrix (Formal Tabular Format)

| Step ID | Description | Actor | Trigger | Inputs | Outputs | Notes / Alternative Paths |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **S01** | **Customer Requests Service:** The customer contacts the auto parts store (in-person at the counter, via telephone customer service, or through the online warranty portal) and formally reports that a previously purchased part is defective and malfunctioning. | Customer | Customer discovers defect or operational failure in recently purchased auto part | Defective auto part details, customer statement of malfunction, contact details | Service replacement ticket logged; initial claim registered | Multi-channel initiation. Basic flow starts here. System/clerk prompts for proof of purchase. |
| **S02** | **Customer Provides Purchase Details:** The customer presents documentation verifying the transaction, specifically providing the store sales receipt, order invoice number, or registered loyalty account details. | Customer | Clerk or online portal requests proof of purchase | Physical paper receipt, digital receipt, or online Order ID | Verification documents submitted into POS / Portal | Required precondition: Must provide sufficient data to locate transaction in store records. |
| **S03** | **Purchase & Policy Validation:** The store clerk enters the order identifier into the system. The system queries the sales database to confirm transaction authenticity, item SKU, purchase date, and eligibility within the return/replacement policy window. | Store & Inventory System / Store Clerk | Submission of purchase identifier (receipt / order number) | Order ID / Receipt, Sales Database, Warranty & Return Policy rules | Validation status: Verified (Proceed) or Unverified (Alternative Flow A2) | **Alternative Flow A2 (Purchase Unverified):** If customer cannot produce proof of purchase or order is past return window, request is denied. Clerk explains documentation policy. Flow terminates. |
| **S04** | **Store Verifies Defect Claim:** The store clerk conducts an objective physical examination of the returned part (or technical support inspects high-resolution digital photographs/video evidence for online portal claims) against manufacturer defect standards. | Store Clerk | Successful completion of purchase verification (S03) | Defective physical part or uploaded digital evidence, manufacturer defect inspection criteria | Defect inspection result: Confirmed Manufacturing Defect or Evidence of Misuse | **Alternative Flow A3 (Part Shows Signs of Misuse):** If inspection reveals physical damage caused by improper tool use, incorrect vehicle installation, or abnormal wear, request is rejected. Customer is reminded of policy exclusions. Flow terminates. |
| **S05** | **System Checks Inventory:** The inventory management system runs an automated real-time query across the local store stock and central distribution warehouse to determine whether an identical or certified equivalent replacement part is available. | Store & Inventory System | Confirmation of valid manufacturing defect from inspection (S04) | Validated part SKU, inventory database, warehouse catalog | Inventory availability status: In Stock or Out of Stock | **Alternative Flow A1 (No Inventory Available):** If identical or equivalent replacement is unavailable, system generates 3 options: (1) Full Refund, (2) Store Credit, or (3) Back-Order. Customer chooses; clerk executes transaction. Flow terminates. |
| **S06** | **Replacement Part Issued & Handed Over:** The store clerk initiates the zero-dollar replacement transaction in the POS system, retrieves the new part from inventory stock, inspects packaging, and hands the replacement over to the customer while retaining the defective part for manufacturer RMA return. | Store Clerk / Customer | Confirmation of in-stock replacement part (S05) | Available new part from stock, validated exchange authorization, customer acceptance | New replacement part in customer custody, defective part quarantined for vendor RMA | Basic flow fulfillment step. Physical possession of replacement part transitions to customer. |
| **S07** | **Confirmation Provided & Workflow Closed:** The system logs the completed exchange, decrements store inventory, registers the RMA quarantine status, and generates a formal replacement confirmation (printed receipt or automated confirmation email with transaction tracking number). | Store & Inventory System | Completion of replacement transaction and part handover (S06) | Completed transaction data, customer email / POS printer | Printed replacement receipt or digital email confirmation; updated ledger | Process completes successfully. Customer departs with working part and proof of warranty exchange. |
