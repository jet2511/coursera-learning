# Practice Assignment: Leverage Gemini Notebook to Prepare for an Interview

## 📌 Tóm Tắt Bài Tập Thực Hành
- **Mục tiêu:** Tận dụng Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) để xây dựng không gian làm việc tập trung (*dedicated workspace*), chuẩn bị toàn diện cho buổi phỏng vấn bằng cách kết hợp thông tin bài đăng tuyển dụng (JD), nghiên cứu công ty và sơ yếu lý lịch (Resume).
- **Quy trình thực hiện:**
  1. Mở [Gemini Notebook](http://notebook.google.com/) và tạo Notebook mới có tên *"Interview Prep Resources"*.
  2. Tải lên các nguồn tài liệu (*sources*): Link hoặc text bài đăng JD, website công ty, tin tức báo chí, báo cáo thường niên và tài liệu cá nhân (Resume, Cover Letter, Career Identity Statement, kinh nghiệm dự án).
  3. Sử dụng khung Chat để yêu cầu Gemini Notebook đề xuất câu hỏi phỏng vấn theo mô hình STAR dựa trên Resume:
     - *Prompt mẫu*: `Please suggest three interview questions I might be asked when applying for the Entry-Level Cybersecurity Analyst job at [Company], and suggest how I might answer them using the STAR method based on my resume.`
  4. Trích xuất danh sách **FAQ** trong mục *Notes* để nhanh chóng nắm bắt các chi tiết quan trọng nhất.
  5. Tạo **Audio Overview** trong mục *Studio* (tùy chỉnh thời lượng/nội dung) để nghe ôn tập mọi lúc mọi nơi.
  6. Khi chuẩn bị cho phỏng vấn mới: Tạo Notebook mới hoặc bỏ chọn (*deselect*) các tài liệu của công ty cũ trong mục *Sources* để tránh nhầm lẫn thông tin.

## 📝 Gợi Ý Trả Lời Chi Tiết (Nộp Bài Coursera)

### Question 1:
> **Prompt:** Write three additional prompts for Gemini Notebook that could help you prepare for an interview. Submit each prompt in Gemini Notebook and save any useful outputs to your job search portfolio. Remember to also save these prompts in your prompt library for future use!

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):

**Prompt 1 (Technical Skill Gap Analysis):**
> *"Based on the uploaded job description and my resume, identify 3 technical areas or toolsets where my experience might appear less aligned with the role's requirements (e.g., SIEM, cloud security, or scripting). Suggest how I can address these potential gaps during the interview using relevant transferable skills and lab projects."*

**Prompt 2 (Tailored Questions for the Interviewer):**
> *"Analyze the uploaded company research documents and recent news articles. Generate 4 thoughtful, strategic questions I can ask the interviewer at the end of the session that demonstrate a deep understanding of their current security posture, team priorities, and tech stack."*

**Prompt 3 (STAR Story Alignment & Technical Terminology):**
> *"Review my attached project notes and resume. Help me draft a structured STAR response for a behavioral question about handling a security incident under pressure (e.g., detecting unauthorized access or malware outbreak), ensuring it seamlessly integrates technical terms like Splunk, log analysis, and incident containment."*

### Question 2:
> **Prompt:** Beyond the applications we've already discussed, how else could you strategically leverage NotebookLM to significantly enhance your job search process? What specific uses do you envision for its capabilities?

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):
Beyond interview preparation, I envision leveraging NotebookLM's source-grounded capabilities in three strategic ways to enhance my overall job search process:

1. **Resume & Cover Letter Keyword Optimization Engine:**
   - *Use case:* I can create a notebook containing 5–10 target job descriptions alongside my master resume. NotebookLM can instantly analyze all JDs to extract recurring technical keywords, required tools (e.g., Wireshark, Splunk, Python), and soft skills, allowing me to tailor my resume for ATS compliance with high precision.

2. **Job Offer & Company Comparative Analysis:**
   - *Use case:* When receiving multiple offers or evaluating different companies, I can upload offer letters, benefits summaries, Glassdoor employee review summaries, and cost-of-living data into a single notebook. I can prompt NotebookLM to generate a side-by-side comparison matrix evaluating salary, growth opportunities, company culture, and work-life balance.

3. **Continuous Technical Upskilling & Certification Prep Workspace:**
   - *Use case:* I can upload technical documentation, framework guidelines (such as NIST SP 800-53 or MITRE ATT&CK), and certification study guides (e.g., Security+). NotebookLM can act as an interactive tutor, generating custom practice quizzes, flashcards, and audio overviews to help me continuously upskill while searching for jobs.
