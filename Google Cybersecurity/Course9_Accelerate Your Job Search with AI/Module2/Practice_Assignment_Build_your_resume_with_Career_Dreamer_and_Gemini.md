# Practice Assignment: Build Your Resume with Career Dreamer and Gemini

## 📌 Tóm Tắt Bài Tập Thực Hành
- **Mục tiêu:** Sử dụng tính năng *Jump to Gemini* của công cụ **Career Dreamer** để tạo bản thảo CV gốc (*Base Resume*).
- **Quy trình:**
  1. Mở Career Dreamer, bấm *Restore* phiên sử dụng cũ.
  2. Bấm *Jump to Gemini* -> Chọn *Workshop a Resume* -> Copy prompt và dán sang Gemini.
  3. Dán thêm *Career Identity Statement* để Gemini viết phần *Summary / About Me* ấn tượng.
  4. Xuất bản thảo sang Google Docs (Export to Docs) và tiến hành rà soát theo tiêu chí: Tính chính xác, kỹ năng chuyển đổi, công thức X-Y-Z, ngôn ngữ ngành và giọng văn cá nhân.
  5. Lưu *Base Resume* vào *Job Search Portfolio*.

### Question 1:
> **Prompt:** If you already had a base resume prior to this activity: How does the new version, drafted with Gemini using your Career Dreamer information, compare to your previous one? For instance, what are its key strengths or improvements, what elements (if any) did your old resume capture more effectively? If you were starting from scratch, how effective was Gemini in generating a solid first draft, and did this AI-supported approach make the task of creating a resume feel more manageable or provide a better starting point than you anticipated?

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):
Compared to my previous resume, the new version drafted with Gemini using my Career Dreamer information is significantly more structured, professional, and targeted. 

- **Key Improvements:** The new version excels at reframing my past non-cybersecurity responsibilities into clear transferable skills. Gemini helped format my accomplishments using strong action verbs and structured bullet points that match current industry standards.
- **AI-Supported Approach:** Starting with Gemini made the process remarkably manageable. It eliminated the "blank page" anxiety and provided a solid, polished first draft in minutes. It gave me a much better starting point than I anticipated, allowing me to focus on refining details rather than building the structure from scratch.

### Question 2:
> **Prompt:** During the personalization phase, which sections of the resume initially drafted by Gemini did you find benefited most from your revisions to best reflect your unique professional story and qualifications? What key changes or additions did you make in these areas?

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):
During the personalization phase, two sections benefited the most from my human revisions:

1. **Professional Summary (About Me):**
   - *Revisions:* While Gemini generated a strong summary, I revised it to incorporate my authentic voice and ensure it clearly reflected my specific career identity statement and passion for entering the cybersecurity field.

2. **Work Experience Bullet Points (Applying the X-Y-Z Formula):**
   - *Revisions:* Gemini drafted general statements, so I added specific quantitative data and context (Y) and detailed the exact tools/methods used (Z). For example, I refined a generic line into a quantified bullet point: *"Resolved 95%+ of technical support tickets within SLA limits by applying systematic troubleshooting and clear client communication."*

3. **Skills & Certifications:**
   - *Revisions:* I explicitly added technical tools, hands-on lab experiences, and specific certificates (such as the Google Cybersecurity Certificate) to align directly with industry expectations.
