# Practice Assignment: Craft Your Career Identity Statement with Career Dreamer

## 📌 Tóm Tắt Bài Tập Thực Hành
- **Mục tiêu:** Sử dụng công cụ **Career Dreamer** của Google để xác định các kỹ năng chuyển đổi (transferable skills), tự động tạo bản thảo *Career Identity Statement* (Tuyên bố bản sắc nghề nghiệp), sau đó tinh chỉnh và tích hợp vào tài liệu tìm việc (Portfolio, Resume, LinkedIn bio, Cover letter).
- **Lưu ý:** Bài tập thực hành này không tính điểm vào điểm tổng kết khóa học.

---

## 📝 Gợi Ý Trả Lời Chi Tiết

### Question 1:
> **Prompt:** Now that you have a refined career identity statement, identify at least two specific ways you plan to use it. How do you believe it will enhance your professional communication in those contexts?

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):
I plan to use my refined career identity statement in the following two specific ways:

1. **LinkedIn Profile Summary / Professional Bio:** 
   - *Application:* I will feature this statement at the top of my LinkedIn "About" section and online portfolio.
   - *Enhancement:* It provides recruiters and hiring managers with an immediate, clear snapshot of my core technical capabilities and transferable skills (such as problem-solving and analytical thinking). Instead of reading a dry resume, they quickly understand my unique value proposition and professional background.

2. **Cover Letters and Elevator Pitches:**
   - *Application:* I will integrate this statement into the opening paragraph of my cover letters and use it as my baseline introduction ("Tell me about yourself") during networking events or job interviews.
   - *Enhancement:* It ensures my communication is concise, confident, and focused. It bridges my past experiences with my current career goals in cybersecurity, helping me articulate *why* I am a strong fit for the role in a compelling way.

---

### Question 2:
> **Prompt:** Beyond the final statement, what is one new insight you gained during this activity – perhaps about your transferable skills, how to frame your experiences, or your overall professional narrative?

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):
One major insight I gained from this activity is how effectively non-traditional backgrounds and past non-cybersecurity experiences can be reframed into valuable transferable skills. 

Previously, I focused primarily on technical hard skills. Through this exercise, I realized that skills developed in previous roles—such as communication under pressure, attention to detail, and navigating ambiguity—are highly sought after by employers in cybersecurity. Framing my professional narrative around these transferable skills gives me greater confidence to present myself as a well-rounded candidate with a unique and strong problem-solving mindset.
