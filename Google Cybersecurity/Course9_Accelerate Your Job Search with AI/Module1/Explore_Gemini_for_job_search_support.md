## 2. Gợi Ý Bảng Trả Lời Self-Assessment (Dành cho vị trí Cybersecurity Analyst)

### **Question 1:**
**Đề bài:** *What was one specific response or idea from Gemini that you found particularly helpful for your job search? What sparked your interest about this idea, and how do you envision applying it in your job search?*

**Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh):**
> One response from Gemini that I found exceptionally helpful was its breakdown of how to tailor my entry-level Cybersecurity Analyst resume for specific job descriptions using keywords from the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. What sparked my interest was Gemini's ability to map my previous transferable technical skills (like SQL and Linux basics) directly to specific security domains such as Incident Response and Asset Management. I envision applying this by using Gemini to analyze cybersecurity job postings, identify skill gaps, and optimize my resume bullet points with relevant industry metrics before submitting each application.

### **Question 2:**
**Đề bài:** *What was your initial reaction or feeling about using a generative AI tool to assist in your job search?*

**Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh):**
> Initially, I felt a mix of curiosity and skepticism about using generative AI for my job search, wondering if the generated responses would feel too generic or artificial. However, after experimenting with structured prompts in Gemini, I was impressed by how efficient it is at brainstorming strategies, drafting outreach messages, and structuring interview preparation plans. It feels like having a personal career coach available 24/7, which boosts my confidence and significantly reduces the time needed for repetitive tasks in the job search process.
