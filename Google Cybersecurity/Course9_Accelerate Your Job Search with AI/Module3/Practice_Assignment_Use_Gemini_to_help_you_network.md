## 📝 Gợi Ý Trả Lời Chi Tiết (Nộp Bài Coursera)

### Question 1:
> **Prompt:** Which networking strategies were the most useful to you, and why? Share three ways you will apply those suggestions to your own job search this week.

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):
The most useful networking strategies Gemini suggested were **Conducting Informational Interviews**, **Engaging in Specialized LinkedIn Groups**, and **Sending Personalized Cold Outreach Messages**. 

These strategies resonated with me because they focus on genuine curiosity, mutual interest, and structured low-pressure conversations rather than generic "networking pitches."

Here are three concrete ways I will apply these suggestions to my job search this week:
1. **Send 3 Targeted LinkedIn Requests:** I will reach out to three Entry-Level SOC Analysts or Security Engineers on LinkedIn with a personalized connection message highlighting a shared interest in cybersecurity and learning about their career path.
2. **Request an Informational Interview:** I will prepare 3–4 thoughtful questions and request a short 15-minute virtual informational interview with a cybersecurity professional to learn about their daily workflow.
3. **Engage in Industry Communities:** I will join two active cybersecurity LinkedIn groups (e.g., focused on Blue Teaming or Security Operations) and actively participate by commenting on recent industry discussions or sharing relevant study notes.

### Question 2:
> **Prompt:** What was your experience like using Gemini to brainstorm networking strategies? Did its suggestions offer new perspectives or make the idea of networking feel more manageable for you?

#### 💡 Gợi ý trả lời (Tiếng Anh - Dùng để nộp bài):
Using Gemini to brainstorm networking strategies was an empowering and eye-opening experience. Prior to this exercise, networking felt intimidating and transactional. 

Gemini helped reframe networking as a process of building mutually beneficial professional relationships rooted in shared learning and curiosity. Key highlights of the experience include:
- **Actionable Templates:** Gemini provided clear message templates and opening lines for cold outreach, which instantly reduced my fear of "sending the wrong message."
- **Structured Framework:** Breaking networking down into manageable steps (e.g., identifying connectors, preparing 3 talking points, following up with a thank-you note) made the entire process feel structured rather than overwhelming.
- **Fresh Perspectives:** Gemini introduced the concept of offering value in return—even as a job seeker—such as sharing relevant industry articles or study insights, making interactions feel far more balanced and natural.
